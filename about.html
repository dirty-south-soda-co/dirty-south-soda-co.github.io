Our story

It started with a TikTok.

Dirty South Soda Co. began with one TikTok video and one tiny trailer.

Lauren started showing up at local events, churches and businesses, hoping people would love her creations. And y'all showed up — laughing, trying crazy flavors and sharing your favorites. That love from the community kept us going.

Since opening in May, we've grown faster than we ever expected: two trailers, a permanent spot at Tanger Outlets, and a menu that now includes refreshers, energy drinks, lemonade, hot chocolate and individually wrapped ice cream — all in our signature "dirty style."
