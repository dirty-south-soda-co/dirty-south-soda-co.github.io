Good to know

Questions? Answers.

What is a dirty soda?

A soda mixed with flavored syrups and a splash of cream. Sweet, creamy and fizzy all at once.

Where are you located?

Our permanent spot is Tanger Outlets Myrtle Beach 501, 4635 Factory Stores Blvd, Myrtle Beach, SC 29579. Our mobile trailer location is posted weekly on Facebook.

What are your hours?

We're open every day, Monday through Sunday, 11am to 7pm.

Do you have sugar-free options?

Yes! We offer sugar-free and syrup-free options, including sugar-free blue raspberry, raspberry, cherry and vanilla flavors, plus zero-sugar soda bases.

How much are drinks?

Dirty sodas are $6 for 16 oz and $8 for 24 oz. Energy drinks run $7–$9, kids' drinks $5–$7 and dirty popcorn is $10. See the full menu.

Can I order ahead?

Yes! Order online for curbside pickup, or get delivery through DoorDash.

Do you sell gift cards?

We do! Send an eGift card online in just a few minutes.

Do you do private events?

Yes! We have two trailers for parties, churches, businesses and community events. Send an inquiry or call (843) 653-6919.
